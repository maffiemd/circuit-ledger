What this is: a hand-curated seed list, not a live feed. Each society publishes its own dates and venue — there is no shared API across academic conferences the way there is for, say, road races. Every row here traces back to the society's own site; rows marked verify on site mean the date, venue, or both were still unconfirmed at last check.

Tracked bodies span the Academy of Management network and its regional chapters (AOM, EAM, MWAM, SWAM, SMA, WAM), continental and national academies (EURAM, BAM, EGOS, ANZAM, IACMR, AAOM, AFAM, CLADEA, the Iberoamerican Academy, NFF, VHB, the Irish Academy, AEDEM, INDAM, ASAC, ANPAD, JABA, AIB's South Asia and MENA chapters, the Turkish National Management Congress, EuroMed Academy of Business, MEEA), labor & employment relations and labor-studies bodies (LERA, ILERA, BUIRA, ILPC, ALERA, AIRAANZ, CIRA/ACRI, the BSA's Work, Employment and Society conference, WCSA, LLRN, ISLSSL, SSLH, LAWCHA, LRAN, the ILO's Regulating for Decent Work network, the ESA's and DGS's Sociology of Work sections, IREC, ISLE, GLU, AASLE, ALAST, LERASA, SASA, JIST, AFS, AIDLASS), HR & organizational-psychology associations (SIOP, EAWOP, AHRD and its regional chapters, ICAP, the BPS Division of Occupational Psychology, EAOHP, GfA, ANPAD's EnGPR conference, AGRH, JAIOP, KSIOP, NAOP, HRIC), conflict-management and socio-economics bodies (IACM, SASE), and the strategy and federation bodies SMS and IFSAM. Also tracked: labor economics (SOLE, EALE), labor history and studies (NALHC, UALE, the Southern Labor Studies Association), sociology with a work focus (ISA, ESSHC, ASA, TASA), critical and process organization studies (ICMS, SCOS, PROS, GWO), occupational health and work-family psychology (Work, Stress & Health, WFRN, the APS IOP conference, AOWI, the Dutch HRM Network, SIOPSA), national societies in Japan and Korea, and a few adjacent or partly practitioner events (AIB, IABS, ILA, EBEN, Labor Notes, GCHRA) that are flagged in their notes.
